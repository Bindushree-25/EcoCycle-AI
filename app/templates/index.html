04 / THE REPAIR-FIRST MINDSET

Before you recycle,
consider what’s next.

Electronics contain materials and components worth recovering. The best option depends on condition, data privacy, battery safety, and local collection rules.

Explore the learning hub ↗
01⌁

Protect your data

Back up and erase personal data before donating, reselling, or handing over a device.

02✳

Respect battery hazards

Do not puncture, crush, charge, or ship a damaged or swollen battery as ordinary waste.

03↻

Keep value in use

Repair, reuse, donate, or resell working devices when practical and safe.

04⌖

Check local rules

Acceptance rules vary. Confirm the item type and opening details directly with the collection service.
